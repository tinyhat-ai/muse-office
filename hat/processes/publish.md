# How <project> runs — Publish

Lead: Marketer. Visuals: Designer.

## Steps

1. Plan — the Marketer proposes what to say and where, from the content plan.
2. Write — the Marketer drafts it in the user's voice.
3. Design — the Designer makes the visual.
4. Your OK — the user approves it (waiting on you).
5. Publish — the Marketer posts or sends it, and logs what it brought in.

## Done when

It is out, and the result is on the Reports page.
