# How <project> runs — Follow up

Lead: Sales. Invoices: Bookkeeper.

## Steps

1. Capture — add the person or update them on the Customers page, with their stage.
2. Next step — set what happens next and when.
3. Draft — on the day, draft the follow-up or the proposal.
4. Your OK — the user approves it (waiting on you).
5. Send and log — send, log it on the person's timeline, and move their stage.

## Done when

The touch is logged, the stage is right, and the next step is set (or "none").
